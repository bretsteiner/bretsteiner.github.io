The Journey of a Pack

From Warehouse Shelves to Happy Families


📦
Step 01

The Great Intake

We track by Category & Weight to keep the warehouse moving fast. No individual barcodes, just bulk efficiency.

1

Categorize

Group items into families like "Pulses" or "Tinned Veg."

2

Weigh-In

Place the category crate on the scale for a total Kg count.

3

Log Entries

Record Weight + Category into the Stock Entry tab.

✅

Provisions Ready

Stock is reconciled, categories are filled, and the warehouse is ready for distribution.

👋
Step 02

A Warm Welcome

The Referral Process: We sit down with our neighbors to understand their unique needs and bring them into the family.

1

Family Count

We log the total humans: How many adults? How many children?

2

Preferences

Crucial check for Dietary Needs (Allergies, Halal, Veg) and Pets.

3

Registration

Data is entered into the Beneficiary Database to set up the rotation.

👤

New Beneficiary Record

A unique Hero ID is born. The system is now ready to calculate their first pack.

🗓️
Step 03

The Master Plan

The system runs a "Logic Check" against our 36 formula templates to design a bespoke 3-month support roadmap.

1

The Magic Math

System maps Household Size + Diet against 36 unique provision templates. This will be manually checked and edited.

2

Collection Dates

Calculates all collection slots for the next 90 days (extendable if needed).

3

The First Pack

Since the interview is live, the first collection date is set to "Today."

📋

3-Month Provision Plan

We now know exactly what to pack and when to pack it for the next 12 weeks.

🛒
Step 04

The Treasure Hunt

Turning the plan into a package! We use reusable tools to guide our volunteers through the warehouse shelves.

1

The Laminated Guide

Grab the template card matching the beneficiary type. Add uniue pick ID, beneficiary name & manual tweaks with a dry-wipe marker.

2

Box Filling

Volunteer follows the card, placing items in the container. Ticks are made directly on the laminate!

3

Identify & Hold

Boxes are labeled. If for a future date, a paper slip is added and the box joins the 'Waiting' stack.

4

Update Stock

The laminated sheets are collected and the unique number from each one is entered into stock control acconting for any changes to the plan.

📦

Packaged & Ready

The physical parcel is now complete, clearly labeled, and waiting in the Distribution Zone. The picked stock is no longer listed in the stock control

🏠
Step 05

The Handover & Loop-back

The final mile! We combine the prepared pack with fresh goods and close the loop on our data.

1

Distribution

Hand over the packed bin plus fresh/frozen items (not practical to log).

2

The "Un-taken" Bin

If a family declines an item, it goes into the Restock Bin. No item is wasted!

3

Shift Reconciliation

At shift-end, we count the restock bin by category and log it as "Inbound," just like Step One.

🔄

System Reconciled

The family is supported, and the Stock Control is perfectly accurate for the next team.

📊
Step 06

Reporting & Alerts

Data-driven insights to manage donor relationships and prevent stockouts.

1

Donation Reports

Total weights received per Donor and in total over custom time periods.

2

Low Stock Alerts

Calculated based on current weight minus estimated 3-month provisioning.Low Stock Report

3

Proactive Plan

Visualizing upcoming deficits to trigger donation requests early.

📈

Intelligent Oversight

Management has total visibility of incoming generosity and outgoing demand.

Step 07

Plan B (Resilience)

Offline operational capability to ensure service never stops if the digital system fails.

1

Analog Pick

Use laminated cards with hand-written demographics and IDs.

2

Label Power

Standalone label printers speed up manual marking of laminate sheets.

3

Occasional Print

Emergency printer for unique template changes when stock fluctuates.

💪

Guaranteed Continuity

The community is served regardless of technology uptime or system status.
