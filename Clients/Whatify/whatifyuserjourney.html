💡 Journey One

The Ideator

You have a spark. A business idea, a book concept, an event waiting to happen. This is how you go from "what if…" to a polished, validated idea with real community backing.

Step 01 — Discovery

The Landing Page

You stumble across What If I through social media, a friend's share, or a search for "how do I validate my business idea." You arrive curious, maybe a little cautious. The platform makes its value clear immediately.

😮 Curious & Cautious
Compelling headline that speaks directly to you
Browse featured ideas to understand the vibe
Success stories from founders like you
A single, inviting CTA: Submit Your Idea
🔢
📋
Step 02 — First Commitment

Idea Submission

A beautifully simple form — just enough to capture the heart of your idea without overwhelming you. You choose what type of "What If" you're asking the world.

🤔 Hesitant but Hopeful
Choose your idea type: Business, Book, Event, Cause…
Working title & short description (under 500 words)
What does success look like for you?
Safe, low-pressure — no NDA yet, just the idea
Step 03 — Joining the Community

Account Creation & Community Vibe

Create your account with a quick social login. Then, before anything goes live, you read and agree to the Community Vibe — a human, readable set of values that protects everyone's ideas in this space.

😤 "I hate signing up…" → ✅ That was easy
Google / social sign-in for speed
Community vibe page — values, not legalese
NDA-style protection in plain language
LinkedIn profile to build credibility
🔐
🎯
Step 04 — Your Command Centre

Ideator Dashboard

Your home base. Track all your ideas at a glance. Each idea has its own Idea Readiness Score — a living indicator of how developed and ready your concept is to share with the world.

🌱 Oriented & Building Momentum
Idea cards with completeness scores (Seed → Grove)
Audience engagement metrics at a glance
To-do list: what to do next to grow your idea
Milestones calendar and submission history
Step 05 — Developing the Idea

Idea Clarifier & AI Business Coach

Using a conversational AI guide, you work through the key dimensions of your idea — the problem, your solution, market size, and business model. The AI doesn't replace your thinking; it unlocks it.

🧠 Focused & Discovering New Angles
AI-led interview style Q&A to flesh out your idea
Business services checklist: marketing, legal, finance
MBA-style frameworks made simple and visual
Idea Readiness Score updates with every step
🤖
✨
Step 06 — Going Public

Your Pitch Deck & Idea Page

Everything you've built comes together in a polished, public-facing pitch deck and idea page. Share it with the world and start attracting your first Inspirators — the people who believe in you before anyone else does.

🚀 Proud & Ready to Share
Auto-generated pitch deck with AI polish
Beautifully designed public idea landing page
Share assets for social media — LinkedIn, Instagram
Start collecting votes, comments, and commitments
Step 07 — Building Your Audience

Engage & Grow Your Community

Your idea is live. Now you nurture it. Respond to Inspirators, see who's backing you and why, understand the demographic breakdown of your supporters, and use their feedback to sharpen and evolve your idea.

💬 Energised by Real Feedback
Comment thread and direct responses to supporters
Demographic breakdown of who is backing you
Import your own contact list to rally your network
Run polls and surveys to get deeper insights
📣
🌳
Step 08 — Execution

From Idea to Reality

Once your idea has traction, the platform guides you toward action — forming your team, formalising the business, finding partners, and connecting with investors. Your idea has become a venture.

🌿 Confident & Moving Forward
Entrepreneur aptitude assessment & strengths profile
Investor introductions when the time is right
Marketplace to find co-founders and collaborators
Merge ideas with complementary Ideators

⚡ Journey Two

The Inspirator

You're a curious explorer, a tastemaker, an early adopter. You love discovering what's next before it's mainstream — and your opinion actually shapes the future of new ideas.

Step 01 — Discovery

Finding What If I

A post appears in your LinkedIn feed. A friend shares an idea link. You click out of curiosity — and land on an idea page that's genuinely interesting. No commitment required to browse.

👀 Curious, casual, low-stakes
Browse ideas freely — no account needed to explore
Pinterest-style discovery feed of live ideas
Category filters: Business, Books, Events, Causes
Featured ideas, trending concepts, recent launches
🔍
✍️
Step 02 — Joining

Quick Registration & Community Vibe

You click to support or comment — and are invited to join. A quick social login gets you in within seconds. You're introduced to the Community Vibe page: why this place exists, how to behave, what makes it different.

🤝 Welcomed & Safe
Google / social login — takes 15 seconds
Select your interests so we surface relevant ideas
Community values page — positive, constructive, real
You choose: just browsing or active Inspirator
Step 03 — First Interaction

React, Vote & Follow

You see an idea that makes you lean forward. You give it a 🔥 for hot idea, a 💡 for clever, a ❤️ for passion. You follow the idea and the Ideator. You're part of something before it's famous.

🔥 Entertained & Inspired
Nuanced reactions — not just a like button
1–10 sliding scale for more honest feedback
Follow an idea or follow an Ideator
See your vote reflected in real-time on the page
❤️
💬
Step 04 — Going Deeper

Comment & Engage

You have something real to say. You leave a comment — sharing personal experience, asking a question the Ideator hasn't considered, or simply saying "keep going, this matters." The AI checks tone to keep things constructive and kind.

🧢 Helpful & Heard
Leave public or private comments on ideas
Tag others, reply to threads, upvote comments
AI sentiment check ensures constructive tone
Six Thinking Hats labels: Creative, Critical, Optimist…
Step 05 — Building Your Profile

Inspirator Identity

Your participation reveals who you are as a thinker. The platform builds your Inspirator profile — your contribution archetype, thinking style, and Scout Score — a measure of your real-world influence and insight quality.

🏆 Finding Your Tribe & Identity
Scout Score: reputation based on upvoted comments
Contribution archetype: Visionary, Strategist, Catalyst…
Badge system tied to depth of engagement
Leaderboard and community ranking
⭐
📢
Step 06 — Spreading the Word

Share & Advocate

You add "Community Contributor at What If I" to your LinkedIn bio. You share an idea you believe in with your network. Your endorsement carries real weight — and your Scout Score grows with every new follower you bring to a project.

📣 "I have good taste. People should know."
Share ideas with personalised "I supported this" posts
Invite your contacts to follow ideas you believe in
FOMO-driven milestones: "247 people said yes!"
Scout Score boosts for bringing new supporters
Step 07 — Your Dashboard

Inspirator Hub

Your personal feed of ideas you're following — what's trending, what's new, what you've commented on. Notifications when an Ideator responds. A curated view built around your interests and your contribution style.

🗺️ Invested & In the Loop
List of all ideas you're following and supporting
Trending ideas filtered by category, stage, location
Notifications for replies, milestone hits, new updates
Profile completeness score and Human Score (anti-bot)

Become an Ideator
