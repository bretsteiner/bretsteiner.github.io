Step 03

Onboarding Meetings

Feeling: 😌 Heard & Validated

Application Status: 📝 Onboarding

We meet up to three times to deeply understand the household's unique story and specific requirements.

Needs Discovery

Detailing dietary needs, allergies, and household preferences.

Documents

Collect, record and store documents. Proof of ID, Address and Eligibility.

Wellbeing

Offer Wellbeing advice and support. Arrange follow up if needed.

Distribution

Create Food Plan and Collection/Delivery Schedule.

📂

Profile Completed

Beneficiary moves into the permanent People spreadsheet (database).

Log requirements for Well-being and Distribution.

⏱️ 45 Mins per Session
Ethnicity, Age Groups, Allergic Triggers, Group preferences.
Photo ID, Proof of Address, Income Verification.
🤷 IF THEY DONT SHOW
Rearrange meeting up to three times before sending an e-mail.
What data is kept or logged at this stage?
⏳ Finalizing: 48 Hours for Activation
