⚙️ How to configure the app via the spreadsheet
Add / rename / reorder items Tab 4 · Stock Ledger — edit col A (name) & col E (section)
Change section colour Tab 4 · Stock Ledger — edit col D (hex colour)
Change portion size or unit Tab 5a · Item Config — cols D, E, F, G
Add misc item suggestions Tab 12 · Misc Items — add rows in col A
Add delivery sources / donors Tab 10 · Reference Data — col A (sources), col C (donors)
Set stock minimum thresholds Tab 4 · Stock Ledger — col F (Min Threshold)
Quantity conversion: Items entered in the Delivery Log are automatically converted from delivery units (kg, bags etc.) to distribution portions using the ratios in Tab 5a · Item Config. The converted portion quantity is what gets saved to the Delivery Log and used by the Stock Ledger formulas. The original delivery quantity is recorded in the Notes column.
