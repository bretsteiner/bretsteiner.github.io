How the OBA System Works

This guide explains every action you can take in the system — in plain English. For each thing you do, it tells you exactly what gets saved, what gets calculated, and what changes on screen.

📖 Getting Started

System Overview

The OBA Operations Suite is made up of two parts that work together: a set of apps you use on screen, and a Google Spreadsheet that stores all the data.

📱
The Three Apps
What each app is for
▾

You interact with the system through three separate apps. Each one talks to the same Google Spreadsheet in the background.

AppWhat it does
📋 Plan CreatorCreate pick plans for beneficiaries, update statuses (Planned, Prepared, Collected, Complete), and add new beneficiaries to the register.
🚛 Delivery LogLog incoming deliveries, do stock takes, and record expected deliveries that have been arranged but not yet arrived.
🖥️ DashboardA read-only overview of everything — picks for the day, stock levels, deliveries, and the 8-week stock forecast.
💡
All three apps need you to sign in with Google first. This is so the system knows who made each change — your name is recorded automatically on every entry.
📊 The Spreadsheet

The Spreadsheet

Everything the apps save ends up in the Google Spreadsheet. It has 12 tabs, each with a specific job. The apps write to the spreadsheet for you — you do not need to type into it directly.

1. Beneficiary DatabaseOne row per person. Stores names, household size, dietary needs, and collection group.
2. Pick PlanEvery item in every pick ever created. One row per item line. Written by the Plan Creator app.
3. To-Do List (2 Weeks)An auto-generated view of what needs doing in the next fortnight.
4. Stock LedgerThe master stock list. Calculates current stock, committed stock, and forecast automatically from the other tabs.
5. Delivery LogEvery delivery ever received. One row per item per delivery. Written by the Delivery Log app.
6. Expected DeliveriesDeliveries that have been booked in but not yet received.
6. Stock Forecast (8 Weeks)Shows predicted stock levels week by week for the next eight weeks, based on planned picks and expected deliveries.
7. Admin DashboardKey summary numbers visible directly inside the spreadsheet.
8. Post-Pick Audit LogRecords any amendments made to picks after they were saved.
9. Template ConfigSets the default pack quantities for each household size (e.g. 1 Adult, 4 People, 6+ People).
10. Reference DataLists of sources and donor names used in the dropdown menus.
11. Stock Take LogEvery stock take ever done. One row per item counted.
🔒
Most tabs are protected — you cannot edit them directly in the spreadsheet. The apps write to them on your behalf. Only a small number of cells (like Min Threshold in Tab 4) can be changed directly.
📦 Stock

Doing a Stock Take

A stock take is when you physically count how much of each item you have on the shelves and enter that number into the system. It resets the starting point for all stock calculations.

📱
Stock takes are done in the Delivery Log app. Tap "Stock Take" from the home screen.
1
You open the Stock Take screen
What loads and what you see
▾
The app reads Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) to get the full list of all items, their sections, and current stock levels.
Items are shown grouped by section — for example, Breakfast and Drinks, Tinned Goods, Store Cupboard. Each section has its own colour, loaded directly from the spreadsheet.
Each item shows its current calculated stock level as a reference. You type in the actual quantity you have just counted on the shelf.
✅
You do not have to count every single item. You can leave items blank if you are only counting certain sections. Only items you enter a number for will be saved.
2
You enter quantities and tap Save
What gets written to the spreadsheet
▾
The app generates a unique Stock Take ID — for example ST-2026-03-19-7342. This ties all the counted items from this session together.
For every item you entered a quantity for, the app writes one new row to Tab 11 (Stock Take Log).
Each row records: Stock Take ID, row number, the date and time, your name, the item name, its section, the quantity you counted, and the item code.
→ Tab 11: Stock Take Log Auto: timestamp added Auto: your name recorded
3
Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) updates automatically
How the current stock figure is recalculated after a stock take
▾

Tab 4 does not store a fixed number for stock. Instead it calculates the current stock level every time using the following logic:

# Current Physical Stock (Column F in Tab 4) Latest stock take count for this item + Deliveries received on or after that stock take date − Picks marked Prepared, Collected, or Complete where the status was changed after the stock take date
💡
This means stock levels are always live. The moment you save a stock take, every item you counted is used as the new starting point. Any deliveries or picks that happened after the count are automatically added or subtracted on top.
🚛 Deliveries

Logging a Delivery

When stock arrives at OBA, you record it in the Delivery Log app. This tells the system that more items are now available and adds them to the stock count.

📱
Deliveries are logged in the Delivery Log app. Tap "New Delivery" from the home screen.
1
You choose the source and donor
Community Drive, Supermarket Collection, or Individual Donor
▾
You pick the type of source — for example: Supermarket Collection, Community Drive, or Individual Donor.
You then pick or type the donor name. The donor list is loaded from Tab 10 (Reference Data). If a donor is not on the list, you can add them on the spot.
You enter the expected delivery date (if you knew it in advance) and the actual date you received it today.
2
You enter the items and quantities
Counting out what arrived
▾
The app shows the full item grid, grouped by section — the same layout as the stock take screen.
You type in how many of each item arrived. Items you did not receive can be left at zero.
A running total updates as you go, showing the overall quantity recorded so far for this delivery.
3
You tap Save
What gets written to the spreadsheet
▾
A unique Delivery Ref is generated — for example DEL-1773882741.
For each item with a quantity above zero, the app writes one row to Tab 5 (Delivery Log).
Each row contains: Delivery Ref, row number, source, donor name, expected date, received date, weight, item name, section, quantity, any notes, and the item code.
→ Tab 5: Delivery Log Auto: Delivery Ref generated Stock Ledger updates instantly
✅
As soon as the delivery is saved, Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) adds those quantities to the current physical stock figure for each item — because the received date is filled in, the formulas include it straight away.
+
Logging an Expected (Future) Delivery
Booking in a delivery before it arrives
▾
From the Delivery Log home screen, tap "Expected Deliveries" to log a delivery that has been arranged but not yet arrived.
You enter the source, donor, expected date, rough weight, and any notes. You do not enter item quantities at this stage — just the overall expected delivery.
This is saved to Tab 6 (Expected Deliveries) and shows with a ⏳ Pending status.
When the delivery arrives, you mark it as ✅ Received. The system then includes it in the stock forecast calculations.
⚠️
Expected deliveries do not add to current physical stock until they are marked as Received. They only appear in the 8-week forecast as expected incoming stock.
📊 Stock Ledger

Reading the Stock Ledger

Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) is the master list of all stock. It shows four different numbers for every item and updates automatically whenever anything in the system changes.

📊
The five columns explained
What each number means
▾
ColumnWhat it shows
E — Min ThresholdThe minimum amount you want to have in stock at all times. You can edit this number directly in the spreadsheet. It is the only column in Tab 4 that is unlocked.
F — Current PhysicalWhat you have on the shelf right now. Calculated from: latest stock take + deliveries received since then − picks that have been Prepared, Collected, or Completed after the stock take date.
G — CommittedStock that is already earmarked for picks in Planned status. It is not gone yet — but it is spoken for and should not be used for anything else.
H — ForecastedA forward-looking number: Physical stock + expected deliveries still to arrive − Committed (Planned) picks. Shows where you are heading.
I — RAG StatusA colour-coded health indicator. See the RAG guide section for what each colour means.
🔴🟡🟢 Status Indicators

RAG Status — What the Colours Mean

Every item in the stock ledger has a RAG status. RAG stands for Red, Amber, Green. It tells you at a glance whether stock levels are healthy or need attention.

⚫
No Data
No stock take has been done for this item yet. The system does not have a counted figure to start from, so it cannot calculate a reliable stock level.
🔴
Below Minimum
Current physical stock is lower than the minimum threshold set in column E. Action is needed — try to restock this item as soon as possible.
🟡
Low Stock
Current stock is above the minimum, but less than one-and-a-half times the minimum. Stock is getting low — keep an eye on it and plan to replenish soon.
🟢
OK
Stock is at a healthy level — at least one-and-a-half times the minimum threshold. No action needed.
💡
The Dashboard shows a summary badge next to the Stock navigation item — for example 3🔴 means three items are currently below their minimum. The badge turns purple if any items are critical.
📅 Forecast

The 8-Week Stock Forecast

The forecast (Tab 6) shows a prediction of how much of each item you will have at the end of each of the next eight weeks, based on planned picks and expected deliveries.

📅
How the forecast is calculated week by week
What goes into each week's predicted number
▾
# Week 1 (the current week, Monday to Sunday) Current Physical Stock (from Tab 4 col F) − Planned picks scheduled within this week + Expected deliveries due within this week (not yet received) # Week 2 onwards Previous week's forecast figure − Planned picks scheduled in that week + Expected deliveries due in that week
⚠️
The forecast only includes picks with a Planned status. It does not include Prepared, Collected, or Complete picks. It also only includes deliveries with a blank received date — meaning they have not arrived yet. It is a prediction, not a guarantee.

In the spreadsheet, cells shown in red mean that week's predicted stock is below the minimum threshold. Cells in amber mean stock is getting low that week.

In the Dashboard's Forecast view, you can hover over any cell to see exactly which picks and deliveries are pushing the number up or down that week.

🛒 Pick Plans

Creating a Pick Plan

A pick plan is a list of items to pack for a specific beneficiary on a specific date. The system works out the right quantities automatically based on their household size.

📱
Pick plans are created in the Plan Creator app. Select a beneficiary from the home screen to begin.
1
You select a beneficiary
What loads when you tap a name
▾
The app reads Tab 1 (Beneficiary Database) to load the person's details — their household size, any dietary needs, and which collection group they belong to (Group 1 or Group 2).
It reads Tab 9 (Template Config) to find the correct default quantities for their household size — for example, a household of four people gets different amounts than a single adult.
It reads Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) to get the current list of all items, their sections, and section colours.
A pick plan is built showing every item with the suggested quantity already filled in. You can see the whole plan before saving anything.
2
You review and adjust quantities
Changing items before saving
▾
Each item shows its suggested quantity based on the template. You can change any quantity by tapping on it and typing a new number.
You can set an item to zero to exclude it from this pick — for example if you are out of that item or the beneficiary has asked not to receive it.
You set the scheduled date — the date the beneficiary is due to collect or receive their parcel.
⚠️
If any quantities differ from the template, the app will show a Mismatch Notice before you save — a reminder that this pick differs from the standard. You can still go ahead and save as normal.
3
You tap Save Plan
What gets written to the spreadsheet
▾
A unique Pick Code is generated — for example MAR26-042 (27). This is the reference for this pick. The number in brackets is the beneficiary's row number in the database.
For every item in the plan, the app writes one row to Tab 2 (Pick Plan).
Each row contains: Pick Code, row number, beneficiary ref, first name, scheduled date, item name, section, quantity, item code. Status is set to Planned.
Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) immediately updates the Committed figure (column G) to include these newly planned quantities.
→ Tab 2: Pick Plan Auto: Pick Code generated Status set to Planned Committed stock increases
🔄 Status Journey

The Pick Status Journey

Every pick plan moves through a set of statuses from the moment it is created to when it is finished. Each status change updates the stock calculations.

📋
The five statuses and what they mean
And how each one affects the stock figures
▾
StatusWhat it meansEffect on stock
Planned The pick has been created but not yet packed. It is in the queue. Counts as Committed stock — reserved but not yet taken off the shelf.
Prepared The parcel has been packed and is sitting ready for collection. Items are deducted from Physical stock. Removed from Committed.
Collected The beneficiary has come in and collected their parcel themselves. Deducted from Physical stock (if not already done at Prepared stage). Removed from Committed.
Complete The pick is fully done — delivered, confirmed received, or closed off. Deducted from Physical stock (if not already done). Pick is included in served history.
Cancelled The pick will not happen — beneficiary could not collect, or pick is no longer needed. Released from Committed stock. Physical stock is unchanged — nothing was packed.
✅ Status Change

Marking a Pick as Prepared

You mark a pick as Prepared when the parcel has been physically packed and is ready and waiting on the shelf for the beneficiary to collect.

→
You tap "Prepared" on a pick in the Plan Creator
Exactly what happens when you change the status
▾
The app updates the Status column in Tab 2 (Pick Plan) from Planned to Prepared for all rows belonging to this pick code.
The Status Changed Date column (column M) is filled in with today's date and time automatically.
The Packed By field (column F) is filled in automatically with the name of the person who is signed in to the app.
Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) recalculates: these item quantities are now deducted from Physical Stock (column F) and removed from Committed (column G).
→ Tab 2: Pick Plan (status updated) Auto: Status Changed Date filled Auto: Packed By filled Physical stock decreases
💡
Once marked Prepared, the stock is treated as used. The Stock Ledger will show these items as no longer available — even though the parcel has not yet been collected. This is the correct behaviour: the stock has physically left the shelf.
📬 Status Change

Marking a Pick as Collected

You mark a pick as Collected when the beneficiary has come in and taken their parcel away themselves — a collection rather than a delivery.

→
You tap "Collected" on a pick
What happens in the spreadsheet
▾
The app updates the Status column in Tab 2 to Collected for all rows in this pick code.
The Status Changed Date (column M) is updated to now.
If the pick was previously Planned (it was never marked Prepared), the items are deducted from Physical Stock at this point — because they have now physically left the building.
If the pick was already marked Prepared, the stock was already deducted. This status change just moves it to the next stage in the journey.
→ Tab 2: Pick Plan (status updated) Auto: Status Changed Date updated
🏁 Status Change

Marking a Pick as Complete

Complete means the pick is fully finished — the parcel has been delivered, confirmed received, or the whole process is done in whatever way applies to that beneficiary.

✓
You tap "Complete" on a pick
What happens in the spreadsheet
▾
The app updates the Status column in Tab 2 to Complete for all rows in this pick code.
The Status Changed Date (column M) is updated to now.
If the pick was still at Planned status (was never Prepared or Collected), the items are deducted from Physical Stock now.
The pick will now appear in the Served This Month view and historical pick records on the Dashboard.
→ Tab 2: Pick Plan (status updated) Appears in served history
❌ Status Change

Cancelling a Pick

If a beneficiary cannot collect, or a pick is no longer needed for any reason, you cancel it. This releases the reserved stock back into the available pool immediately.

✕
You tap "Cancelled" on a pick
What happens to stock when a pick is cancelled
▾
The app updates the Status column in Tab 2 to Cancelled for all rows in this pick code.
The Status Changed Date (column M) is updated.
Because Cancelled picks are not counted in the Committed calculation, the quantities are immediately released. Tab 4's Committed figure (column G) goes down, freeing up that stock for other picks.
Physical stock is not changed — the items were never packed or taken off the shelf.
→ Tab 2: Pick Plan (status updated) Committed stock released Physical stock unchanged
⚠️
Important: If a pick was already marked Prepared before being cancelled, the stock has already been deducted from the physical count — because the parcel was packed. In that case, you should do a fresh stock take to bring the figure back up to the correct level.
✏️ Amendments

Amending a Pick

If you need to change the quantities in a pick after it has been saved — for example if an item is out of stock — you can use the Amend function in the Plan Creator app.

✏️
You open the Amend panel
What you can change and what gets recorded
▾
Tap the expand arrow on any pick in the Plan Creator to open its detail view, then tap Amend.
You can change the quantity for any item in the pick. You can also add a note to explain the reason for the change.
When you save, the app updates the Qty column (column L) in Tab 2 for each amended row.
A record of the amendment is also saved to Tab 8 (Post-Pick Audit Log) with the updated quantity, your name, and the reason given.
Tab 4 recalculates — the Committed or Physical stock figures update to reflect the new quantities.
→ Tab 2: Pick Plan (qty updated) → Tab 8: Post-Pick Audit Log Timestamp and user name recorded Stock recalculates with new qty
🖥️ Dashboard

The Dashboard

The Dashboard gives a live overview of everything happening across the system. It reads from the spreadsheet in real time. Nothing in the Dashboard can be edited — it is a read-only view.

🗓️
The day strip at the top
How selecting a day controls what you see
▾
At the top of the Dashboard is a 7-day strip showing the days of this week. Today is highlighted.
Tapping a different day updates the whole dashboard — the overview cards, the picks list, and the day heading all change to show that day's information.
The top bar greeting updates too — for example: "Good Morning — Wednesday 25 Mar". The picks count in the top bar also updates to show the selected day's total.
📊
The four overview cards
What each card on the main screen shows
▾
CardWhat it shows
Parcels — [Selected Day]How many collections and how many deliveries are scheduled for the day you have selected in the strip.
Picks and PreparationHow many picks need preparing today, how many are already ready, a progress bar showing completion, and a warning flag if any picks are overdue.
Stock SnapshotA live summary of items that are critical (🔴 below minimum) or low (🟡 getting low), shown with colour-coded dots. Tap "View all stock" to see the full list.
Deliveries In — TodayAny confirmed deliveries that were logged as received today.
📅
The Forecast view in the Dashboard
How to use the 8-week grid
▾
Tap 📅 Forecast in the bottom navigation bar to open the 8-week forecast grid.
The current week's column is highlighted with a coloured left border so you can find it instantly.
Items are grouped by section with dark header bands between them, matching the same layout as the other views.
Cells highlighted in red mean that week's predicted stock falls below the minimum threshold. Amber cells mean stock will be getting low.
Hover over any cell (on a computer) to see a tooltip showing exactly which picks are deducting stock that week (shown in amber) and which deliveries are adding to it (shown in green).
🏷️
The navigation badges
The numbers next to the nav icons
▾

🆘 Emergency

Emergency Backup Form

The Backup Form is for emergencies only — for example if the main apps are unavailable, the internet is down, or you cannot sign in to Google. It lets you log stock takes and deliveries in a simplified way.

🆘
Only use this form if the main apps are not working. It writes directly to the spreadsheet with fewer safety checks. Use the Plan Creator and Delivery Log apps for all normal day-to-day operations.
!
What the Backup Form can do
Its two emergency functions
▾
ModeWhat it doesWrites to
Emergency Stock Take Enter counted quantities for any items. Works exactly like the normal stock take but through the simplified form. Tab 11 (Stock Take Log)
Emergency Delivery Log Record a delivery that has arrived. Enter the source, donor, and quantities received. Tab 5 (Delivery Log)
⚠️
The Backup Form loads its item list and source list directly from the live spreadsheet, so it is always up to date even in an emergency. It still requires a Google sign-in to work.
🔑 Reference

Item Codes

Every item in the system has a unique code. These codes are used behind the scenes to make sure all calculations are accurate — even if an item's display name is ever slightly changed.

🔑
How item codes work and the naming convention
Why they exist and how they are structured
▾

Item codes follow a prefix + number format. Each section of the stock list has its own prefix:

PrefixSectionExample codes
BRKBreakfast and DrinksBRK-001, BRK-002, BRK-003...
TINTinned GoodsTIN-001, TIN-002, TIN-003...
STCStore CupboardSTC-001, STC-002, STC-003...
FFVFree From / Vegan / VegetarianFFV-001, FFV-002...
TOIToiletriesTOI-001, TOI-002...
BABBaby ProductsBAB-001, BAB-002...
CLNCleaning ProductsCLN-001, CLN-002...
💡
Item codes are stored in Tab 4 (Stock Ledger) column B and written to every other tab automatically by the apps. You never need to type them yourself — the system handles everything. You will see them in Tab 2, Tab 5, and Tab 11 as confirmation that the record has linked correctly.
🔒 Security

Sheet Protection

Most tabs in the spreadsheet are protected (locked). This stops accidental edits and keeps the data clean and accurate. The apps write to the protected tabs on your behalf.

🔒
What is locked and what you can edit directly
A summary of which cells you can type into in the spreadsheet
▾
TabWhat you can edit directly
4 — Stock LedgerOnly column E (Min Threshold). You can change the minimum stock level for each item here whenever needed.
9 — Template ConfigColumns D to I — the quantity values per household size. Change these to update the default pack amounts across all future picks.
10 — Reference DataRows 4 onwards. Add or edit donor names and delivery sources here.
1 — Beneficiary DatabaseMost columns except Ref No. (column A, which is auto-set). Dropdown menus are provided for Group, C/D, PR/DR, Frequency, Status, Dietary, and Allergies.
All other tabsFully locked. Written only by the apps. If you need to correct data in these tabs, speak to the system administrator.
💡
The admin password for protected sheets is available from the OBA team. It should only be used by the system administrator and not shared outside the operations team.
