Brand Voice

✓
Warm and practical. OBA tools should feel reassuring and efficient — never clinical or corporate. Use plain language, not jargon.
✓
Dignified and discreet. Beneficiary data is displayed with care. Avoid alarmist language around sensitive information.
✗
Never cold or bureaucratic. Avoid “user record”, “null value”, “data entry required”. Prefer “beneficiary”, “no information yet”, “add details”.
