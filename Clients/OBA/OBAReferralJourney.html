Step 01

Initial Reach Out

Feeling: 😟 Overwhelmed but Hopeful

Application Status: 📝 Referred

Potential beneficiaries or their support network reach out through trusted channels to enter the OBA safety net. Direct referrals come via the OBA website — the applicant finds the site, navigates to Get Help, and selects Direct Referral for Individuals to begin an 8-sheet web form.

🏥 Professional Referral

Social workers, schools, energy companies, housing associations and partner orgs (e.g. St Mungo's, St Mary's, Reay School) refer via a dedicated form. OBA works directly with the referring org.

🙋 Direct Self-Referral

Individual fills in an 8-sheet web form: name, address, borough, household composition, ethnicity, dietary needs, allergies, sanitary items, pets, and a free-text circumstances note.

🔄 Extension / Wellbeing Only

Existing beneficiaries nearing end of their support cycle, or those needing wellbeing support only (housing advice, clothing, signposting) without food parcels.

📥

Centralized Intake

Form data auto-populates the Direct Referrals Spreadsheet. An alert email is sent to the OBA Referrals inbox. Sonya, Vespina, Harley and Kris have access.

Step 02

Application Review & Triage

Feeling: ⏳ Anxiously Waiting

Application Status: 📝 Under Review

The OBA team checks the referrals inbox daily (SLA: within 48 hours). The spreadsheet is opened, new applications are reviewed, validated and triaged. Colour coding distinguishes auto-populated fields from manually added data.

✅ Borough Check

Confirm applicant is in one of the 5 serviced boroughs: Lambeth, Wandsworth, Suffolk, Croydon + surrounding areas. If out-of-area → redirect email to local services.

✅ Person Match

Manually check if applicant has been helped before. Must be 6+ months since last referral. Exceptions: hospital admission, medical reason, unemployed but not entitled to benefits.

✅ Borough Balance

Check the current mix of beneficiaries across boroughs. If balance is off (e.g. not enough Lambeth support), others may be waitlisted until balance is restored.

✅ Referral Type Check

Confirm the application is truly a direct referral and not a professional accidentally using the wrong form (e.g. a Birmingham address is a red flag).

🚨 Emergency / Complex Cases

If the circumstances note flags domestic violence, rough sleeping, health crisis or other urgent need — immediate phone call is made. If situation is very severe, refer on to other organisations immediately before offering an appointment.

📅 Arrange Appointment

Find a slot in the Appointments Schedule Google Sheet. Mondays only, 3 people per 15-min slot, up to 20 appointments. In-person is standard; online only for mobility/medical exceptions. Record operator name and response date.

📧

Appointment Offered via Email (or SMS if no email)

Templated email sent with appointment details and list of documents to bring. Different templates for: out-of-area · waitlisted · previous recipient (6-month query) · in-person invite · online invite. Phone calls always followed up by email.

📊 Google Sheets Used at This Stage
Direct Referrals Spreadsheet — read to validate; operator name & response date written to row Appointments Schedule (Google Sheet) — checked & slot confirmed; appointment booked into Monday slots
💡 Process Improvement Suggestions
⚠️
No visible "To Do" flag when a referral arrives: Add a dedicated column "Action Status" with values: New / In Review / Appointment Sent / Waitlisted / Closed. Any row sitting on "New" for more than 48hrs is immediately visible to anyone opening the sheet — another team member can pick it up without being told.
⚠️
Appointment booking is manual and invisible to others: Add a column "Appointment Offered Date" and "Appointment Slot" to the Direct Referrals Spreadsheet that is filled the moment a slot is booked. If blank, anyone can see the task is incomplete.
⚠️
No confirmation email is tracked: Add a column "Invite Email Sent? (Y/N)" to the Direct Referrals Spreadsheet. Staff tick it after sending. If it's blank, a colleague knows the email was never sent and can follow up without duplicating effort.
⚠️
Emergency escalation has no paper trail: Add a column "Emergency Flag" with a dropdown (None / Phone Call Made / Referred On). This ensures that if Sonya is interrupted mid-triage, anyone can see where the case stands.

Step 03

Welfare Interview (The Arch)

Feeling: 😌 Nervous but Welcomed

Application Status: 📝 Onboarding

Beneficiary arrives at the Arch with their documents. They are warmly greeted at the door, offered tea, coffee and access to clothing items while they wait. All referral routes converge here — direct, professional and partner-referred beneficiaries all go through the same welfare interview.

📋 Documents Required

Identity

Photo ID, passport or equivalent for the applicant.

Proof of Address

Utility bills or similar for all adults in the household.

Proof of Children

Birth certificates, passports or Universal Credit account details for children.

Carry-Out

Beneficiary brings their own trolley or bags to collect the food parcel.

🗣️ Interview Topics

Needs Assessment

Confirm household composition (M/F breakdowns, exact ages of children), disabilities, dietary preferences, allergies, pets, sanitary & incontinence items, nappy sizes, bedding.

Financial Situation

Housing debt, credit card debt, mobile phone bills, utility arrears, loan sharks, medical financial stress. Refer complex debt to SHINE or similar.

Mental Health & Wellbeing

Red flag: substance abuse or alcohol (asked to leave → refer to Ace of Clubs). Domestic violence, counselling needs, ongoing check-in support. Refer to Income Max CIC for grants & benefits.

Medical & Existing Support

Mobility issues, long-term treatments, chronic illness, children's testing. Check what organisations they already work with (school, social workers, partners) to avoid duplication.

📂

Profile Completed → Assigned to Group 1 or 2

All notes recorded in the spreadsheet comments. Household info passed to admin (Harley) for distribution planning. Group 1 = first two weeks of month; Group 2 = second two weeks. Collection or delivery flagged ("bags" = delivery). Welcome letter given with contact details, referral info and next appointments. Homework may be assigned (e.g. bring bills to next appointment).

📊 Google Sheets Used at This Stage
Appointments Schedule — checked at door; applicant confirmed against slot list Direct Referrals Spreadsheet — opened during interview; household data, disabilities, allergies, notes written to comments column Beneficiary Sheet — new beneficiary record created; Group 1/2, collection/delivery, case type, open/closed status set
💡 Process Improvement Suggestions
⚠️
All interview notes go into a free-text comments column: This is invisible to anyone reviewing later and easy to miss. Add dedicated columns for each key output: Group Assigned · Collection or Delivery · Disabilities (Y/N) · Referrals Made · Homework Set (Y/N) · Welcome Letter Given (Y/N). Tick-box columns take seconds to fill and mean any team member can see completion at a glance.
⚠️
Household info passed to Harley verbally or informally: Add a column "Passed to Distribution (Y/N)" that the interviewer ticks once they've briefed Harley. If unticked at end of day, the parcel cannot be prepared — this flag catches the gap before it becomes a problem.
⚠️
No-shows are marked at end of day, but who checks? Agree a named person responsible for end-of-day no-show calls. Add a column "Follow-up Call Made (Y/N)" so it's visible if that call was skipped during a busy session.
⚠️
Online interviews have a separate document submission flow (WhatsApp/email) that could be missed: Add a column "Docs Verified & Deleted (Y/N)" for online cases. This also creates a GDPR audit trail.

Step 04

First Distribution & Active Support

Feeling: 💪 Relieved & Supported

Application Status: ✨ Active Beneficiary

After the interview, household and food preference info is passed to the admin team (Harley) who prepares the distribution plan. The beneficiary is called by the picking team when their first parcel is ready — including store cupboard, fresh and frozen items.

📲 SMS Booking

Beneficiary receives an SMS to book their next collection or delivery slot. They must respond promptly to secure their preferred time. Slots fill quickly.

🚚 Collections & Deliveries

Beneficiaries are assigned to Group 1 (weeks 1–2) or Group 2 (weeks 3–4). Delivery ("bags") beneficiaries are allocated a driver in the Collections & Deliveries Spreadsheet.

📞 Ongoing Contact

Beneficiary can contact OBA at any time via phone or email (info page on website). Sophie's OBA phone and email are included in the welcome letter. Track volume of calls and emails.

🤝 Wellbeing Check-Ins

2nd and 3rd wellbeing reviews conducted. OBA can discuss beneficiary's situation with other organisations if a signed authorisation form is in place.

🔁 Extension Process

Near end of support cycle, a template email is sent offering a wellbeing review. If an extension is needed, two staff members must agree. Warning email sent in the second-to-last month. Extensions logged and reviewed.

🏫 Wellbeing-Only Pathway

Beneficiaries who only need housing advice, clothing or signposting (no food) can access ongoing wellbeing support indefinitely with no limits, even within the 6-month food gap period.

✨

Support Cycle Live — 3 Months Standard (Ongoing if Required)

Welcome letter confirmed. Group, schedule and case type (collection/delivery, food/wellbeing, open/closed) all logged. Referrals to Income Max, SHINE, Ace of Clubs or other partners actioned as needed and recorded in reporting.

📊 Google Sheets Used at This Stage
Beneficiary Sheet — active record; Group, schedule, case status maintained Collections & Deliveries Spreadsheet — driver allocated for delivery ("bags") beneficiaries Direct Referrals Spreadsheet — referral notes & partner org referrals logged in comments

Step 05

Follow-up Wellbeing Interview

Feeling: 🤝 Trusting & Opening Up

Application Status: 🔄 Ongoing Support

After the first distribution, beneficiaries return for a deeper wellbeing conversation — either in person at the Arch or via phone. By this point the beneficiary has had time to reflect, and often opens up more fully. This is where the most meaningful support connections are made.

🗣️ Follow-up Interview Topics

🏠 Homework Review

Review bills and financial statements brought in from the homework task. Map debt and identify routes to relief. Refer to SHINE or similar if debt is complex.

💸 Benefits & Grants

Review benefit entitlements. Refer to Income Max CIC if the beneficiary may be missing out on grants, benefits forms or government support they haven't yet claimed.

🧠 Mental & Emotional Wellbeing

Follow up on any flags from the first interview. DV situations, mental health, counselling. Beneficiaries often share more at this stage once trust is established.

📋 Situation Update

Has anything changed? New medical issues, household composition, debt, or employment. Update record. Confirm no duplication with other support organisations.

📝 Authorisation Form

If OBA needs to liaise with another organisation on the beneficiary's behalf, a signed authorisation form is required and collected here if not done earlier.

📅 Next Collection Slots

2nd and 3rd slots communicated. Beneficiary reminded to respond to SMS promptly. Next wellbeing check-in scheduled if needed.

⏳ Extension Review

If approaching end of cycle (month 2), raise extension option. Two staff members must agree to any extension. Duration and options explained to beneficiary.

📋

Updated Record & Ongoing Plan Confirmed

Notes and actions added to spreadsheet. Referrals to partner organisations logged. Authorisation forms stored. Next collection slot confirmed. Extension or closure decision recorded if applicable.

📊 Google Sheets Used at This Stage
Beneficiary Sheet — case notes updated; extension or closure flagged Direct Referrals Spreadsheet — wellbeing notes, referrals and homework outcomes added to comments Collections & Deliveries Spreadsheet — next slot confirmed; delivery driver allocated if applicable
💡 Process Improvement Suggestions
⚠️
Follow-up interview is expected but not formally scheduled: Add a "Follow-up Interview Scheduled (Y/N)" and "Follow-up Date" column to the Beneficiary Sheet at first interview. A blank date signals the task was never booked — any team member can action it without being briefed.
⚠️
Homework outcomes are only in free-text comments: Add "Homework Set (Y/N)" and "Homework Reviewed (Y/N)" columns. If homework was set but review is blank, it's immediately visible as an incomplete task — even if the original interviewer is not in that day.
⚠️
Authorisation forms have no tracking: Add an "Auth Form Signed (Y/N)" column so it's instantly clear whether OBA is legally authorised to speak to another organisation on this person's behalf.
⚠️
No automated reminders between collections: Automate an SMS 24–48hrs before each scheduled collection or delivery. This reduces no-shows and eliminates the manual phone calls currently needed to chase them in a busy environment.
