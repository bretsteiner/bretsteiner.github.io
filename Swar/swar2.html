01 About

I am Swar — a relational psychotherapist.

I am an experienced Transactional Analysis psychotherapist and counsellor with twelve years of experience working with individuals aged 18 and older. My early five years involved working with children in a school setting — giving me a deep understanding of formative experience.

"Saying hello to me is the first step to meeting yourself."

As a relational practitioner, I am passionate about providing the unique relationship each client needs to engage safely and creatively together. With mutual curiosity, my clients can walk through their journeys at their own pace — healing old wounds and becoming the arbiters of their own stories.

03 How it works

Starting therapy with Swar

I meet clients by appointment only — once a week in a recurring 50-minute session on a fixed and agreed day and time.

1
Reach out

Fill out the contact form or email directly. There's no obligation — just an open conversation about whether we might work well together.

2
First session

A 50-minute session to share what brings you to therapy. I'll ask some in-depth questions to understand how I can help. By the end, we'll both have a sense of whether we can work together.

3
Ongoing work

If we're a good fit, we agree a regular weekly slot. Therapy unfolds at the pace that's right for you — there is no fixed timeline.
