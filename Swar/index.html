About Swar

I am Swar —
a relational psychotherapist.

I am an experienced Transactional Analysis psychotherapist and counsellor with twelve years of experience working with individuals aged 18 and older. The initial five years of my practice involved working with children in a school setting.

While some relationships can leave us unsatisfied, depressed or anxious, others can help heal and set us to grow and thrive.

As a relational practitioner, I am passionate about providing the necessary and unique relationship each client needs to engage safely and creatively. With mutual curiosity and my support, my clients can walk through their journeys at their own pace — healing old wounds, learning about themselves, and becoming the arbiters of their own paths.

Areas of Support

What I work
with

I work with a wide range of emotional, psychological, and relational difficulties. Every person's experience is unique, and therapy is tailored entirely to you.

01
Relationship Issues

Navigating unhealthy patterns, conflict, and connection. Understanding why relationships feel difficult — and how to change that.

02
Depression & Low Mood

Working through persistent sadness, lack of motivation, and the heavy weight that can make everyday life feel impossible.

03
Anxiety

Exploring the roots of worry, fear, and overwhelm — and finding new ways to feel safe in yourself and in the world.

04
Trauma & Abuse

Gentle, careful work with past wounds — creating space to process difficult experiences at a pace that feels right for you.

05
Addiction & Substance Misuse

Understanding the needs beneath compulsive behaviours and developing new, healthier ways to meet those needs.

06
Bereavement, Loss & LGBTQ+

Support through grief, identity, and the transitions that shake our sense of self — with compassion and without judgement.

How it Works

Starting
therapy
with Swar

I meet clients by appointment only — once a week in a recurring 50-minute session on a fixed and agreed day and time.

Session Fee
£75
Per 50-minute session, payable in advance.
AXA Health Insurance accepted.
01
Reach out

Fill out the contact form on this page or email me directly to express your interest in beginning therapy. There's no obligation — just a conversation.

02
First session

We meet for an initial 50-minute session. You can tell me about yourself and what brings you to therapy. I'll answer your questions and ask some of my own to begin understanding how I can best help you.

03
Ongoing work

If we both feel we can work together, we'll agree a regular weekly slot. Therapy is a journey — there's no fixed timeline. We go at the pace that's right for you.

04
Cancellation policy

A 7-day notice period is required for cancellations. Sessions cancelled with less notice are chargeable. This helps protect the time we've committed to your therapeutic process.
